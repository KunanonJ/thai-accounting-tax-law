## Keeping books

- **Accounting Act B.E. 2543 (พ.ร.บ.การบัญชี พ.ศ. 2543), s. 14:** keep accounting books and supporting documents for at least **5 years** from the closing date of the books. The Director-General may require up to 7 years [V via RD ruling].
- VAT records: also **5 years** (Revenue Code s. 87/3) [V].
- Standard for non-publicly-accountable entities (most SMEs): **TFRS for NPAEs** (revised edition) [S].
- Supporting documents are the evidence behind every entry: tax invoices, receipts, bank slips, contracts, payment advices. Attach them to the transaction in your accounting software and keep the originals.

## Audit

- Every juristic partnership and company must have its annual financial statements audited.
- **Companies:** always a **CPA** (ผู้สอบบัญชีรับอนุญาต) [S FlowAccount, citing RD].
- **Registered partnerships:** a **TA** (ผู้สอบบัญชีภาษีอากร) is allowed only if capital ≤ 5M THB, assets ≤ 30M THB **and** revenue ≤ 30M THB; otherwise a CPA [S FlowAccount, citing RD].

## Annual calendar for a limited company (31 December year end)

| Step | Deadline | Example for year end 31 Dec 2026 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual general meeting approves audited financial statements | Within **4 months** of year end | by 30 Apr 2027 | [S InfoQuest, citing DBD] |
| Shareholder list (บอจ.5) to DBD | Within **14 days** after the AGM | ~14 May 2027 if the AGM is on 30 Apr | [S InfoQuest, citing DBD] |
| Financial statements to DBD (DBD e-Filing) | Within **1 month** after the AGM | ~30 May 2027 if the AGM is on 30 Apr | [S InfoQuest; S Matichon] |
| ภ.ง.ด.50 with audited financial statements | Within **150 days** of year end | 30 May 2027 (online: +8 days if the extension is renewed) | [V PwC; V RD FAQ] |

## Registered partnerships (หจก.)

- No AGM requirement. Financial statements go to DBD within **5 months** of year end, e.g. by 31 May for a 31 December year end [S InfoQuest, citing DBD].
- ภ.ง.ด.50 within 150 days, as for companies.

## Good monthly practice (close checklist)

These are practitioner habits rather than legal rules:

- [ ] Record every sale and expense with its supporting document attached
- [ ] Reconcile each bank account to the statement; match at a difference of zero rather than plugging differences
- [ ] File and pay WHT (ภ.ง.ด.1/3/53/54) and, if registered, VAT (ภ.พ.30/36) on time
- [ ] Remit social security contributions
- [ ] Review AR and AP ageing
- [ ] Lock the period in your accounting software once it is reconciled and filed
