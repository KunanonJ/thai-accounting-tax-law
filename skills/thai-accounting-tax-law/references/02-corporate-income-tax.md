# 2. Corporate income tax · ภาษีเงินได้นิติบุคคล

**English** · [ภาษาไทย](02-corporate-income-tax.th.md)

_Last verified 15 Nov 2026. Tags: [V] official · [S] secondary · [U] unconfirmed._

Applies to Thai juristic persons: limited companies, limited partnerships and registered ordinary partnerships.

## Rates

- **Standard rate: 20%** of net profit [V PwC].
- **SME rates** apply only if **both** hold: paid-up capital ≤ **5M THB** at the end of the accounting period, **and** income from sales of goods and services ≤ **30M THB** in that period [V PwC]:

| Net profit (THB) | Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 300,000 | 0% |
| 300,001 – 3,000,000 | 15% |
| above 3,000,000 | 20% |

Check paid-up capital against the latest DBD record before applying SME rates.

### Worked examples (SME)

| Net profit | Calculation | CIT |
|---|---|---|
| 250,000 | all in the 0% band | **0** |
| 1,000,000 | (1,000,000 − 300,000) × 15% | **105,000** |
| 4,000,000 | 2,700,000 × 15% + (4,000,000 − 3,000,000) × 20% = 405,000 + 200,000 | **605,000** |

The same 4,000,000 profit at the standard rate would be 800,000.

## Returns and payment

| Return | Due | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ภ.ง.ด.51** (half-year) | Within **2 months** after the end of the first 6 months | SMEs compute it on half of the estimated full-year profit; tax paid is credited against the annual return [V PwC] |
| **ภ.ง.ด.50** (annual) | Within **150 days** after the accounting period ends | Tax is paid with the return [V PwC; V RD FAQ Q7] |

Online filing gets **8 extra days** for returns due between 1 Feb 2024 and 31 Jan 2027 [V Deloitte, citing the MoF announcement; V rd.go.th]. Check whether it has been renewed for later returns.

- An accounting period shorter than 12 months (e.g. a first year) still needs a ภ.ง.ด.50 [V RD FAQ Q16].
- A company with **no income** must still prepare financial statements and file ภ.ง.ด.50 [V RD FAQ Q6].

## Penalties

- Not filing ภ.ง.ด.50 on time: fine of up to **2,000 THB** [V RD FAQ Q31–32].
- Late tax: surcharge (เงินเพิ่ม) of **1.5% per month or part of a month** on the tax due [V RD FAQ Q31–32]. The surcharge cannot exceed the tax itself (Revenue Code s. 27) [S].

## Audit and assessment

- The RD can summon a taxpayer for audit within **2 years** after filing; longer if there is evidence of evasion. The general limitation is 10 years [V PwC].
