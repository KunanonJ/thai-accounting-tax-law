# 6. Filing calendar · ปฏิทินยื่นภาษี

**English** · [ภาษาไทย](06-filing-calendar.th.md)

_Last verified 15 Nov 2026. Tags: [V] official · [S] secondary · [U] unconfirmed._

"Online" means RD e-Filing. The **8 extra days for online filing** apply to returns due between 1 Feb 2024 and **31 Jan 2027** [V]. If the deadline you are checking falls after that, confirm the extension was renewed. When a deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday it moves to the next business day [S PEAK].

## Every month (for the previous month)

| Day | What | Who | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **7th** (paper) / **15th** (online) | ภ.ง.ด.1, ภ.ง.ด.3, ภ.ง.ด.53: withholding tax returns | Anyone who withheld tax that month | [V RD; S FlowAccount] |
| **7th** (paper) / **15th** (online) | ภ.ง.ด.54: WHT on payments abroad | Anyone who paid abroad and withheld | [V RD; S] |
| **7th** (paper) / **15th** (online) | ภ.พ.36: self-assessed VAT on foreign services | Buyers of foreign services (see chapter 4) | [V RD] |
| **15th** | Social security contributions (5% employee + 5% employer) | Employers | [S FlowAccount] |
| **15th** (paper) / **23rd** (online) | ภ.พ.30: monthly VAT return, even with no sales | VAT-registered businesses | [S] |

## Every year (31 December year end)

| When | What | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| By **end of February** | ภ.ง.ด.1ก: annual summary of employee income and WHT (online ~8 days later) | [S FlowAccount; S Prachachat] |
| By **30 April** | Company AGM approves the audited financial statements | [S InfoQuest, citing DBD] |
| Within **14 days** of the AGM | บอจ.5 shareholder list to DBD | [S InfoQuest, citing DBD] |
| Within **1 month** of the AGM | Company financial statements to DBD | [S InfoQuest; S Matichon] |
| By **31 May** | Partnership financial statements to DBD (5 months) | [S InfoQuest, citing DBD] |
| By **30 May** (150 days; 29 May after a leap-year February) | ภ.ง.ด.50: annual CIT return and payment | [V PwC; V RD FAQ] |
| By **31 August** (2 months after 30 June) | ภ.ง.ด.51: half-year CIT return | [V PwC] |

For a different year end, count the same periods from your own closing date.

## Time-limited measures to watch

| Measure | Ends | What happens if it isn't renewed |
|---|---|---|
| Online filing +8 days | Returns due up to **31 Jan 2027** | Online deadlines fall back to the paper dates |
| e-WHT 1% | **31 Dec 2027** | Payments through e-WHT go back to normal WHT rates |
| VAT 7% | **30 Sep 2027** | VAT reverts to the statutory 10% |
| Social security ceiling 17,500 THB | End of **2028** | Rises to 20,000 THB (2029–2031), then 23,000 THB (2032 onward) |
