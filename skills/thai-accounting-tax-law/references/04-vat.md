# 4. VAT · ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม

**English** · [ภาษาไทย](04-vat.th.md)

_Last verified 15 Nov 2026. Tags: [V] official · [S] secondary · [U] unconfirmed._

## Rate

- **7%** (6.3% VAT plus local tax), kept until **30 Sep 2027** by Royal Decree No. 807 (B.E. 2569), dated 20 Aug 2026, published in the Royal Gazette on 23 Aug 2026, in force from 1 Oct 2026 [V Forvis Mazars; V RegFollower citing the Gazette].
- The **statutory rate is 10%**. The 7% rate has been extended by decree roughly once a year since 1999 [V Nation Thailand]. Before 30 Sep 2027, check that a new decree has been published.

## Registration

- Registration is **compulsory once turnover exceeds 1.8M THB a year**; apply (ภ.พ.01) within **30 days** of crossing the threshold [S FlowAccount; S RD regional article].
- Registering late means back-VAT from the day the threshold was crossed, a penalty of up to 2× the tax and a 1.5% per month surcharge [S FlowAccount].
- Voluntary registration below the threshold is possible [S].

## If you are VAT-registered

- Charge 7% output VAT (ภาษีขาย) and issue **full tax invoices** (ใบกำกับภาษีเต็มรูป) [S].
- Claim input VAT (ภาษีซื้อ) only with a valid tax invoice in your company's name and tax ID [S].
- File **ภ.พ.30** monthly, **even with no sales**: by the **15th** of the following month on paper, or 8 days later online [S; online +8 days V for returns due up to 31 Jan 2027].
- Keep VAT records for **5 years** (Revenue Code s. 87/3) [V].

## If you are not VAT-registered

- Do not charge VAT or issue tax invoices. Use receipts (ใบเสร็จรับเงิน) and invoices instead [S].
- You **cannot claim input VAT**; VAT you pay is part of your cost [S].
- ภ.พ.30 does not apply [S].

## Services bought from abroad (ภ.พ.36)

- Foreign **e-service** platforms registered for Thai VAT (many large SaaS, ad and app-store providers) charge 7% VAT directly to buyers who are not VAT-registered [S FlowAccount].
- A Thai buyer of services performed abroad and used in Thailand may have to **self-assess VAT on ภ.พ.36** and remit it. A VAT-registered buyer can usually claim it back as input VAT the following month [S].
- Whether a **non-registered** business must self-assess ภ.พ.36 on foreign services that were not already charged Thai VAT is **[U]**. Ask your accountant.
- ภ.พ.36 paper deadline: within **7 days** after the end of the month of payment [V RD].

## Quick decision table

| Situation | VAT action |
|---|---|
| Turnover will pass 1.8M THB this year | Register within 30 days of crossing it |
| Registered, buying from a Thai VAT-registered supplier | Keep the tax invoice; claim input VAT on ภ.พ.30 |
| Registered, paying a foreign service provider not charging Thai VAT | Self-assess ภ.พ.36; claim it back as input VAT |
| Not registered, paying a foreign platform that charges Thai VAT | Nothing to file; the VAT is part of the cost |
| Not registered, paying a foreign provider that charges no Thai VAT | **[U]**: ask whether ภ.พ.36 applies |
