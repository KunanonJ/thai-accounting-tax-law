# 1. Business entities · รูปแบบธุรกิจ

**English** · [ภาษาไทย](01-business-entities.th.md)

_Last verified 15 Nov 2026. Tags: [V] official · [S] secondary · [U] unconfirmed._

## The common forms

| Form | Legal personality | Income tax | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sole trader (บุคคลธรรมดา) | No | Personal income tax | None required |
| Ordinary partnership (ห้างหุ้นส่วนสามัญ, unregistered) | No | Personal income tax, paid by the partnership as one unit | None required |
| Registered ordinary partnership (ห้างหุ้นส่วนสามัญนิติบุคคล) | Yes | Corporate income tax | CPA, or a TA if small (below) |
| Limited partnership (ห้างหุ้นส่วนจำกัด, หจก.) | Yes | Corporate income tax | CPA, or a TA if small (below) |
| Private limited company (บริษัทจำกัด, บจก.) | Yes | Corporate income tax | **Always a CPA** |

A juristic partnership or company files financial statements every year and must keep proper books under the Accounting Act (see [chapter 5](05-accounting-audit-dbd.md)).

## Who may audit: CPA vs TA

- **Companies always need a CPA** (ผู้สอบบัญชีรับอนุญาต) [S FlowAccount, citing RD].
- A **registered partnership** may use a tax auditor (TA, ผู้สอบบัญชีภาษีอากร) instead, but only if capital ≤ 5M THB, total assets ≤ 30M THB **and** revenue ≤ 30M THB [S FlowAccount, citing RD].
- So when a หจก. converts into a บจก., it loses the option of a TA from the first company year [S].

## Converting a หจก. into a บจก. (แปรสภาพ)

**Civil law.** Civil and Commercial Code ss. 1246/1–1246/7 let a registered partnership convert into a limited company. The company takes over all the partnership's assets, debts, rights and liabilities. The partnership is not liquidated or deregistered separately [V CCC].

**Tax rulings.** Two Revenue Department rulings treat a conversion as **neither a dissolution nor an amalgamation** for CIT purposes (Revenue Code ss. 72–74):

- กค 0702/8805 (22 Oct 2009) and กค 0702/5646 (20 Jul 2009): moving the assets into the company is **not taxable income** and **not a sale** (s. 39). So there is no CIT, **no WHT** (s. 69 ter), **no specific business tax** and **no stamp duty** when title is re-registered [V].
- The rulings are from 2009. They do **not** settle the accounting period, whether a final partnership ภ.ง.ด.50 is due, the tax ID, VAT registration or loss carry-forward [V — scope of the rulings].

**Practitioner guidance (conflicts with the rulings' logic; unresolved).** Some practitioners advise: prepare financial statements as at the conversion date with a special audit, have the company file the partnership's ภ.ง.ด.50 for the start of the year up to the conversion date within 150 days, and notify banks, the RD and the SSO [S Riskless Accounting]. Whether a stub-period return is needed is **[U]**. Ask your accountant and, if in doubt, request an RD ruling.

**Practical checklist after converting** [S]:

- [ ] New company affidavit (หนังสือรับรอง) and tax ID; update every contract, invoice template and letterhead from the conversion date
- [ ] Tell banks, the RD, the SSO and key customers and suppliers
- [ ] Decide which bank account the company will use; agree with your accountant how to treat money still flowing through the old partnership account
- [ ] Opening balances for the company's books: trial balance, AR/AP lists, fixed-asset register and bank balances as at the conversion date
- [ ] Appoint a CPA for the first company year
- [ ] Use the **document date** to decide which name goes on a document: before the conversion date the partnership, from that date the company
