# 7. Other laws that touch day-to-day operations · กฎหมายอื่นที่เกี่ยวข้อง

**English** · [ภาษาไทย](07-other-laws.th.md)

_Last verified 15 Nov 2026. Tags: [V] official · [S] secondary · [U] unconfirmed._

## PDPA: Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล)

- Report a personal-data breach to the PDPC office within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it (s. 37(4)) [S Legal500].
- Keep security measures appropriate to the risk (s. 37(1)) [S Legal500].
- Appoint a Data Protection Officer where required (s. 41(2)). Practitioners use roughly 100,000 data subjects, or processing personal data as a core activity, as the trigger [S].
- Penalties: administrative fines up to **5M THB**; criminal penalties for some offences of up to 1 year's imprisonment and/or fines up to 1M THB; plus civil damages [S Legal500].
- The first large fine was **7M THB**, announced on 21 Aug 2024 against a private company for failing on DPO appointment, security and breach notification [S Thai PBS].

## Payment Systems Act B.E. 2560 (พ.ร.บ.ระบบการชำระเงิน)

- E-money, card services, collecting payments on behalf of merchants and e-money transfers must be **licensed or registered with the Bank of Thailand**. Operating without one carries criminal penalties [V BOT].
- If your product holds customer value (stored balances, points, cashback wallets), check with a lawyer whether it is e-money before you design or describe it that way.

## Stamp duty · อากรแสตมป์

| Instrument | Duty | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hire-of-work / service contract | 1 THB per 1,000 THB | [S FlowAccount, Oct 2026] |
| Lease | 0.1% of total rent | [S same] |
| Loan | 1 THB per 2,000 THB | [S same] |
| Power of attorney | 10 THB | [S same] |

- Generally pay within **15 days** of signing; e-Stamp Duty is available online [S].
- A document that should have been stamped and wasn't may not be admissible as evidence in a civil case [S].

## Social security · ประกันสังคม

- Employer **5%** + employee **5%** of monthly wages, up to a wage ceiling [S FlowAccount; S Thairath]:

| Period | Wage ceiling | Max contribution each (per month) |
|---|---|---|
| 2026–2028 | 17,500 THB | 875 THB |
| 2029–2031 | 20,000 THB | 1,000 THB |
| 2032 onward | 23,000 THB | 1,150 THB |

- Remit by the **15th** of the following month [S FlowAccount].
- Applies only if you have employees. A newly registered juristic person is registered as an employer with the SSO automatically [S FlowAccount].
