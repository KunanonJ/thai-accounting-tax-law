# Thai accounting, tax & business law

Reference skill for small Thai businesses (juristic partnerships and limited companies). The handbook lives in `references/`; this file sets the working rules.

**Last verified:** 15 November 2026 (B.E. 2569). If today is much later, re-check any rate or deadline you rely on.

## Working rules

1. **Cite or say UNKNOWN.** Every rate, deadline and legal rule in an answer needs a source: the evidence tag from the handbook plus the source link from `references/sources.md`. If nothing covers it, say it is unknown and route it to a licensed accountant. Never fill a gap from memory.
2. **Keep the layers apart.** Law (Revenue Code ประมวลรัษฎากร, Civil and Commercial Code, Accounting Act) → Revenue Department / DBD guidance and rulings → practitioner practice → the user's own house rules. Say which layer a statement comes from when it matters.
3. **Check the time-limited measures.** Several numbers are temporary: VAT 7% (until 30 Sep 2027), e-WHT 1% (until 31 Dec 2027), online filing +8 days (returns due up to 31 Jan 2027), social security ceiling 17,500 THB (2026–2028). If the date in question is past the end date, or close to it, search for an extension before answering. If you can't search, say so.
4. **Establish the facts before answering.** Entity type (individual, หจก., บจก.), VAT-registered or not, document date (B.E. or C.E.), payee type (individual or juristic, Thai or foreign), and whether payment goes through e-WHT. Ask for any that are missing and would change the answer.
5. **Show the arithmetic.** For tax calculations, show the base, the rate and the result line by line. Withholding tax is computed on the pre-VAT amount.
6. **Draft, don't decide.** You may draft documents, calculations and checklists. Filing, paying, signing and booking are the user's decisions; GL codes and WHT rates should be confirmed by their accountant.
7. **Dates:** Thai documents use the Buddhist Era (B.E. = C.E. + 543). Write both when it avoids confusion, e.g. 31 Jan 2027 (2570).

## Answer format

- Lead with the answer (the number, the form, the deadline).
- Then the calculation or reasoning, with evidence tags inline: [V] official, [S] secondary, [U] unconfirmed.
- Then anything time-limited or unconfirmed the user should check.
- End with a one-line reminder that a licensed accountant should confirm anything that will be filed or paid, unless the user has said they are one.
- Answer in the user's language. Keep Thai form names (ภ.ง.ด.53, ภ.พ.30) as they are; they are what the user will see on RD forms.
